- **An earlier full run, on c1918fb,** added the facts behind each rating and matching by billed service, and passed 47 of 48. It is not this scorecard, because the skill changed after it:
  - **`v13-cron-resync-unchanged`** was rated High, as expected, but its dollar figure ($1,678 a month) rested on a catalogue of 10,000 products, where the key assumed 3,000. The repository cannot show the catalogue's size, so the key now says its figure is an assumption (`monthly_usd_assumes`), and the grader does not compare figures that rest on one. This was a key change only.
  - **`s6-cron-with-progress`** failed on two rules that disagreed. A news feed that stores each article once and never deletes it was priced at D1's 10 GB limit (about $19 a month) under "Growth that a size limit stops" and rated Medium, while "A cap is a ceiling, not a rate" says such data follows how fast items arrive. e727cf6 says the size-limit rule is for growth the code causes on its own, and that data the product keeps on purpose is rated by how fast it arrives, with a Low finding recommending retention. On e727cf6, `s6` filed that Low finding with no monthly figure. The same confusion had failed `v15` once in the earlier 1.9.0 run.
- **`s1-bounded-alarm`** failed on e727cf6 on a real issue its key had missed. The presence route allowed one signed-in account 60 pings a minute, and each ping keeps one room's 30-second alarm running for about five minutes. Nothing caps how many rooms one account belongs to, so one account could keep about 300 rooms awake, about $37 a month of Durable Object requests and rows written. Earlier runs priced a single room. fee26d9 lowers the limit to 10 pings a minute, about $0.63 a month at most, and states that worst month in the key. The case passed when run again there, with the agent's own figure at $0.56. The skill is the same on both commits.
