## Notes

- **Which cases ran.** 1.9.0 changes how paths a caller drives are rated, so the run covered every case whose fixture or answer key changed (`s2`, `s3`, `s6`, `s7`, `s15`, `s17`, `s19`, `s20`, `v9`, `v23`, `v26` and the new `v27`, `v28`), the cases whose only change was a lower search rate limit (`v11`, `v14`, `v15`, `v16`), and the signed-in bounded cases whose keys stayed the same (`s1`, `s5`, `s12`, `s14`). The other 27 cases were not run for this release; their last results are in `1.8.0.md`.
- **First run.** All 21 cases ran on d746f2f, three at a time, and 18 passed.
- **`s2-bounded-queue-consumer`** failed on d746f2f on a real issue the answer key had missed: the relay stored each caller-chosen delivery id in D1 forever with no length check, so one leaked token could fill the database to its 10 GB limit, and bodies of 64,000 characters made each message two queue units. The agent priced that at about $9 a month for its worst case and rated it Medium. The fixture was changed in 3d9298a to check the id and to measure the payload limit in bytes, and the case passed when run again there.
- **`v26-monorepo-order-history`** failed on d746f2f although its findings were right. Order creation was rated Medium, which the case's `may_flag` allows, but the finding also cited the receipt insert and the idempotency cleanup, within eight lines of two `must_not_flag` anchors. The grader in 3d9298a judges a finding at a `may_flag` location there first. The same report passes under that grader, and regrading the 1.8.0 Opus and Fable runs with both graders changes no other result.
- **`v15-flag-off-key-scan`** failed on d746f2f on an agent error: a Medium finding on article, embedding and vector growth, priced at the embedding batch ceiling of 432,000 a month, which the skill says not to do when each new item is paid for once. Run again on 3d9298a, it passed. The skill is the same on both commits.
- The two new cases did what they were built for: `v27-agent-rate-limit-only` (gap 1) and `v28-upload-rate-limit-only` (gap 2) were both rated High, and `v9-admin-token-fanout` moved from Medium to High. `v11-cron-no-progress` (gap 3) rated the harvest Low, at the $3.75 D1 ceiling.
